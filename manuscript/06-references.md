## Notes for the author before submission

**1. Verify the IB descriptors against the IBO original.** The ten IB descriptors quoted in §4 were verified against Whitmarsh (2022), an open-access article under a CC-BY licence that reproduces the profile in full. Whitmarsh attributes the profile text to an IB publication dated **2015**, whereas the version distributed by the IB and cited in this manuscript is dated **2017**. The wording is, on all available evidence, identical between them, but the reference should be fixed against the copy you hold, and the date corrected if it differs. Do not leave this to chance: a quotation attributed to the wrong edition of a primary source is the kind of error a reviewer of comparative work will check.
